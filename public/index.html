Cover.AI

An AI-Powered chrome extension for your job hunting needs!

Job Description Collection🛡️

Automatically Parses Job Descriptions from Workday Platforms.

AI Cover Letter Generation 🚀

Uses parsed job description to generate a cover letter based on description and your resume.

Extract Key Job Details 📈

Display's key job details for the user at the top of the extension.

Cover Letter Storage 🦉

Save and download cover letters in PDF format. Easily access and manage your cover letters.

Customizable AI input✨

Augment your AI prompt with additional information about yourself.

Gemini modeling🧰

Our extension uses Google's Gemini LLM.
